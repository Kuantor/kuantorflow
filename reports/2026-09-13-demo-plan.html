The morning of

  • ★ Check the Anthropic credit balance. — the single most likely way the demo goes wrong. Mykola just stops answering, with no error.
  • ★ Hard-refresh after the last deploy (Ctrl+F5). — a cached stylesheet renders a plausible wrong page, not a broken one. You wrote the CSS; you would not notice.
  • ★ Look up one word, confirm translations come back. — the failure that looks like nothing at all.
  • Play each game once, on the deck you will show. — cheaper than any test, and how the last three real bugs were found.
  • Decide which of the four things you are leading with. — the deck, the lookup, the games and Mykola are four different pitches.

What to show, in this order

  1. Generate a text, then the worksheet. Their vocabulary becomes a reading passage, then a printable gap-fill. Lesson preparation — the one feature a teacher would use even if their students never touched the site.
  2. A round URL is already a homework assignment. Paste /games/spell_it/play?topic=Health into a class chat and it is the homework. No code — it just has never been said out loud.
  3. One deck, ten activities. One vocabulary set drilled ten ways, without re-entering the words per exercise.
  4. Notes upload. Their existing word list becomes a deck without typing it in.
  5. Mykola — last. The thing that impresses and the thing most likely to wobble. Lead with the lesson material.
Say the gap before you are asked. A teacher will want to know how their students are doing within five minutes. Nothing records that yet — #338 is the foundation and is not built, and there is no teacher–student relationship at all. Saying so early beats being caught by it.
