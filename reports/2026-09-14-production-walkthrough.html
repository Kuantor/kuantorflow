One open question, answered for free

#147 is not a demo risk. The ticket worries that the General topic holds the oldest, messiest cards — misspelled headwords among them — and that a teacher browsing the deck will find them.

On the deployment there is no General or General knowledge topic visible at all, and /flashcards/General knowledge returns 404 for a visitor. The three non-curriculum topics that are visible were spot-checked and their headwords are clean: appeal, felony, jurisprudence, statute, warrant, castle, wisdom.

So the messy cards are not reachable by browsing. Recommendation: spend nothing on #147 before the demo. That answer cost one page load instead of a production database dump.

Two things I got wrong on the way

I briefly concluded the lookup was silently failing. It was not. Both words I tried were already in the deck, so the app correctly showed its duplicate warning — and I had searched the page for the wrong phrases before deciding nothing had happened. Worth recording because “it does nothing” was exactly the wrong diagnosis, and it is the one this site is most likely to invite.

A near-miss worth knowing about for any scripted interaction here. Going to submit the text-generation form programmatically, my first selector matched the account deletion form from base.html instead — it is rendered on every page. I inspected the form before submitting rather than after, so nothing happened.

Both of these are arguments for the runbook's own line: play it yourself, on the deck you will show. A person clicking the button cannot misidentify which form they are on.
